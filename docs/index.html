05

Guide — 7-day speed plan

📅 Days 1–2 · Lines + systems

Do: graph 3 lines in 45s; solve 5 textbook 2×2 via intersection, plug-verify each. Pass: intersection in ≤25s. Tip: write equations as y=… first — Desmos graphs functions fastest.

📈 Days 3–4 · Quadratics + tables

Do: 5 roots + 5 vertex reads; table-test 5 “which value” Qs; plug x=2 for 10 equivalents. Pass: roots in ≤30s. Tip: vertex question asks y (value), not x — read both coordinates.

🎯 Days 5–7 · Mixed + test day

Do: 5 inequalities + 5 circles (square grid on); mixed 10Q timed, log graph-vs-hand per Q. Pass: decide method in ≤5s. Tip: slow first 10 Qs — adaptive M1 decides your M2 difficulty.
