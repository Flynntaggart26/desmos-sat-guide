5. Full guide (condensed)

60-second rule

Algebra >60s → graph. Desmos >60s (one-step) → hand. Systems, quadratics, equivalents, circles → Desmos first.

7-day speed plan

D1 lines, D2 systems, D3 quadratics, D4 tables, D5 inequalities+circles, D6 mixed timed, D7 self-test. 10 min/day.

Test day

Bluebook updated, charger, paper backup. Slow first 10 Qs (adaptive M1 decides M2). Guess + flag >90s RW / >2 min Math.
