# Quadratics & Functions with Desmos

## Read Answers Off Graph
Type `y=x^2-4x+3` → x-intercepts = roots (1 and 3), bottom point = vertex (2,-1). That’s the answer for most root/vertex Qs — no factoring needed.

Forms:
- Standard `ax²+bx+c` → roots via intercepts, vertex x=-b/2a (verify on graph).
- Vertex `a(x-h)²+k` → vertex (h,k) directly. a<0 opens down (max), a>0 up (min).
- Factored `a(x-r1)(x-r2)` → roots r1,r2.

## Zero Method (any equation)
Bring all to one side, graph `LHS-RHS`, zeros = solutions. Example: `x²-5x+6=0` → `y=x²-5x+6` zeros at 2,3.

## Equivalents (plug x=2 + graph)
Choices look alike? Plug x=2 into Q and each choice (table or type). Survivor = match. Graph survivor vs Q to confirm overlap.

## Transforms
`y=f(x-h)+k`: h right, k up. Inside opposite, outside same. Test: `y=(x-2)^2+1` vs `y=x^2` — shifted right 2, up 1. Slider h/k to feel.

## Exponents
`y=2^x` vs `y=x^2` — graph to see which grows faster, find intersection for “when equal?” Qs.

Trap: vertex y matters (k), not just x. Read both coordinates.
